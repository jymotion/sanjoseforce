Shop Information

When will my order ship?
In-stock items ship within two business days from our San Jose warehouse. You’ll get a tracking number by email as soon as it leaves us.
What is the return policy?
Unworn merchandise can be returned within thirty days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is free on orders over $75.
How do the sizes run?
Tees and fleece run true to size. A full size chart with chest and length measurements is linked on every product page.
Can I pick up my order at the stadium?
Yes. Choose in-store pickup at checkout and collect from the FORCE Team Store at General Electric Field, open weekdays and on all home game days.
