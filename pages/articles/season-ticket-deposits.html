The San Jose FORCE are now accepting deposits for 2026 Season Tickets at General Electric Field.

A fully refundable $50 per-seat deposit secures your place in the seat selection queue, which opens in the spring. Current Season Ticket Members keep their existing seats and their existing pricing, and do not need to place a deposit to renew.

What Membership Includes

  • All seven home games at General Electric Field
  • Priority access to postseason tickets
  • Member-only events throughout the year
  • A dedicated account representative
  • Locked-in pricing for the following season

Pricing for 2026 starts at $49 per game in the Upper Level. A complete price sheet by seating level is available on the Tickets page.

Interest Following the Championship Run

Demand has been strong since the club’s postseason appearance, particularly in the Sideline and Club levels, where availability is already limited. Fans considering a membership are encouraged to place a deposit early.

Deposits are refundable at any point up until seat selection. Questions can be directed to the ticket office at tickets@sanjoseforce.com.
