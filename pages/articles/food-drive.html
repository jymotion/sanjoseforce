The FORCE Foundation’s annual holiday food drive collected a record volume of donations this month, filling every collection bin at General Electric Field.

Fans attending the final home game of the season were invited to bring non-perishable items to any gate. Collection points were staffed by club employees and volunteers from FORCE Nation throughout the day.

Where It Goes

All donations are distributed through regional food banks serving Santa Clara County, with priority given to programs supporting families with school-aged children over the winter break.

“Every year we set a target and every year FORCE Nation clears it. This community shows up.” VP, Community Relations, San Jose FORCE

Thank You

The club extends its thanks to everyone who contributed, to the volunteers who worked the collection points, and to General Electric for underwriting transport and storage.

The FORCE Foundation runs community programs year-round. Details are available on the Community page.
