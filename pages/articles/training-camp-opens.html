The San Jose FORCE opened 2026 training camp at General Electric Field this week, marking the club’s first full-squad on-field work since last December’s Schmeague Championship.

Players reported Monday morning and moved straight into conditioning testing before taking the field for a walkthrough that afternoon. Participation in the voluntary offseason program was the highest in club history, and the coaching staff said the difference was visible from the first whistle.

“This group came back in the best shape I have seen since I have been here. Nobody had to be eased into anything. That is a credit to the work they put in on their own time.” Neil Hobbs, Head Strength and Conditioning Coach, San Jose FORCE

On the Practice Field

The opening week followed the club’s standard camp structure. Sessions begin with a stretch period and individual position work, move into one-on-ones along the line and on the perimeter, and finish with seven-on-seven and full-team install. Special teams periods are worked in twice a week.

Practices have been held in the morning to manage the August heat, with the club’s acclimatization protocol in place for the opening stretch. Pads come on later this week.

“Camp is about competition. Every rep is being evaluated. The standard does not change based on how last season finished.” Dan Whitaker, Head Coach, San Jose FORCE

Open Practices

Several camp practices will be open to the public at General Electric Field. Admission is free, gates open one hour before each session, and Lot A parking is complimentary on open practice days.

  • FORCE Nation members receive advance notice of all open practice dates
  • Buster will appear at every open session
  • Autograph periods follow selected practices, weather permitting
  • The Kids Club zone will operate on the Gate C plaza
  • Standard General Electric Field policies apply, including the clear bag policy

What Comes Next

Camp continues at General Electric Field through the remainder of the summer. The club will publish practice reports as the schedule progresses, and roster information will be shared once it is finalized.

Season Ticket Members will receive first access to all camp events. Full details are available on the Tickets page.
