The San Jose FORCE have qualified for the Schmeague postseason for the first time in club history following a 96.94 to 85.06 road victory over Daddy’s Insight on Sunday.

The win moved the FORCE to 8–5 with one week remaining in the regular season and secured the club’s place in the postseason field.

Three Years in the Making

The FORCE began play as an expansion franchise in 2023. Sunday’s result marks the first time the club has reached the postseason in its three seasons of existence.

“We said in the spring that the standard was the postseason. The group met it.” General Manager, San Jose FORCE

San Jose has been strong away from home all season, improving to 5–2 on the road with Sunday’s win.

Up Next

The FORCE close the regular season at General Electric Field before beginning postseason play. Postseason ticket information will be shared with Season Ticket Members first.
