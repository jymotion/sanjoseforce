The San Jose FORCE will undertake a renovation of the main concourse at General Electric Field beginning in spring 2026, with work scheduled for completion before the home opener.

What Is Changing

  • The walkway behind sections 112 through 128 will be widened by approximately eight feet
  • Four new concession stands will be added, including two dedicated grab-and-go outlets
  • Additional restrooms will be built behind section 120
  • New wayfinding signage will be installed throughout the level
  • The Guest Services desk near section 108 will be relocated and expanded

The project responds directly to feedback gathered from Season Ticket Members, who identified concourse congestion at halftime as the most common frustration on game day.

“Our members told us where the pinch points were. This work addresses them.” Doug Ferris, President of Business Operations, San Jose FORCE

Impact on Fans

No home games are affected. The venue will remain open for scheduled non-football events during construction, with temporary concession locations in place where needed.

Updates will be posted to the Stadium page as work progresses.
