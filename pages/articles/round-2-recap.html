The San Jose FORCE are headed to the Schmeague Championship after a 99.72 to 73.94 victory over The Philly Fakeouts on Sunday.

The win avenged both regular season meetings between the clubs. The Fakeouts, who finished 10–4, opened the season with a 104.86 to 101.12 win over San Jose in Week 1 and took the rematch at General Electric Field in Week 12, 109.72 to 103.88. Both were decided by fewer than six points.

Sunday was not close in the same way. San Jose held its opponent to 73.94, the second time this season the FORCE have kept an opponent under 75 points.

“We had two chances at this club during the year and came up short both times. The group did not forget that.” Dan Whitaker, Head Coach, San Jose FORCE

Second Straight Road Win

The FORCE have now won both of their postseason games away from General Electric Field, following a club-record 147.06-point performance in Round 1. San Jose finished the regular season 5–2 on the road.

Up Next

The FORCE advance to face Bustin Lutz OG, the league’s top club at 11–3, in the Schmeague Championship next Sunday.
