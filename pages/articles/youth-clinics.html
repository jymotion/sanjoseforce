The San Jose FORCE youth football clinic series concluded its fall season this weekend, capping a program that reached more than four hundred participants aged 8 to 14.

Clinics ran across eight Saturdays at fields throughout the greater San Jose area, with sessions covering fundamentals, position basics, conditioning and safe play. All clinics were free to attend and equipment was provided.

Focus on Safe Play

Every clinic included a dedicated segment on safe technique and injury awareness, delivered in partnership with local athletic trainers. Participants and their guardians also received written guidance to take home.

“Most of these kids are not going to play this game forever. What they take with them is how to be a good teammate.” Tim Brennan, Director of Player Personnel, San Jose FORCE

Spring Registration

The spring clinic series opens for registration in February. FORCE Nation members receive advance notice, and spots are typically filled within a week of opening.
