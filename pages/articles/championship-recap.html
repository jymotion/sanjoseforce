The San Jose FORCE fell to Bustin Lutz OG on Sunday in the Schmeague Championship, ending the club’s first postseason run one game short of a title.

The final was 149.88 to 80.22. Bustin Lutz OG, who finished the regular season 11–3 and entered the weekend as the league’s top club, posted the highest single-game total any FORCE opponent has managed this season.

San Jose’s 80.22 was its second-lowest output of the year. The two clubs had met once previously, in Week 9 at General Electric Field, where Bustin Lutz OG won 117.92 to 109.38.

“Credit to Bustin Lutz OG. They were the best club in this league all year and they showed it again on Sunday. Our group has nothing to hang its head about.” Head Coach, San Jose FORCE

A Run to Remember

The FORCE reached the Championship by winning consecutive road games, opening with a club-record 147.06 points against Throat Goats before eliminating The Philly Fakeouts 99.72 to 73.94.

Sunday marked the third postseason game in club history, all of them played in the span of fifteen days.

What Comes Next

The FORCE finish 2025 with a regular season record of 8–6 and a postseason record of 2–1. The club will turn its attention to the 2026 Schmeague draft in the coming weeks.

The organization thanks FORCE Nation for its support throughout the postseason.
