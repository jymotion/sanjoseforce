The San Jose FORCE have acquired quarterback Joe Burrow from Big Bob’s Beepers in exchange for wide receiver Christian Watson, the club announced following the conclusion of the 2026 draft.

The framework of the deal was in place before either player changed hands. Terms were agreed between the two clubs prior to the FORCE selecting Watson, meaning San Jose drafted the receiver already knowing he would be moved.

How the Deal Came Together

The Beepers selected Burrow earlier in the draft, before learning that quarterback Drake Maye remained available. Having then added Maye, the Beepers made Burrow available, and the two front offices reached an understanding within minutes.

“There’s a lot of preparation that goes into a draft, but sometimes it just comes down to luck. [Beepers GM Bob Brown] and I just happened to be seated next to each other at the draft. He drafted Maye, we talked, shook hands and that was that.” Kevin Malcolm, General Manager, San Jose FORCE

The Draft Equity

Burrow was drafted at $10 and Watson at $6. The FORCE therefore come out of the trade $4 to the good in draft equity, having added the more expensive asset while giving up the less expensive one.

What the FORCE Are Getting

Injuries limited Burrow to eight games last season. When healthy, he is regarded by many around the league as a top-five quarterback, and the club believes the position has been upgraded meaningfully for 2026.
