The San Jose FORCE have acquired quarterback Joe Burrow from Big Bob’s Beepers in exchange for wide receiver Christian Watson, the club announced following the conclusion of the 2026 draft. Terms were agreed between the two clubs prior to the FORCE selecting Watson.

How the Deal Came Together

The Beepers selected Burrow earlier in the draft, before learning that quarterback Drake Maye remained available. Having then added Maye, the Beepers made Burrow available, and the two front offices reached an understanding within minutes.

“There’s a lot of preparation that goes into a draft, but sometimes it comes down to luck. [Beepers GM Bob Brown] and I just happened to be seated next to each other at the draft. He drafted Maye, we talked, shook hands and that was that.” Kevin Malcolm, General Manager, San Jose FORCE
“The FORCE are a trusted partner of mine. I’ve known them longer than anyone in the league. It takes two like minded, sharp individuals to conduct a live trade at an auction and that’s what you have here.” Bob Brown, General Manager, Big Bob’s Beepers

High Upside

With Burrow costing the Beepers $10 and Watson costing the FORCE $6, San Jose was able to gain $4 in draft equity, which helped them to land running backs Rico Dowdle and Blake Corum with subsequent picks.

The real-world value of both Burrow and Watson may depend more on health than talent. Injuries limited Burrow to eight games last season. When healthy, he is regarded by many around the league as a top-five quarterback. After passing his physical, the club believes the position has been upgraded meaningfully for 2026.
