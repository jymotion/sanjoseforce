FORCE Nation, the official fan club of the San Jose FORCE, has surpassed 25,000 registered members, the club announced this week.

The milestone comes at the close of a season in which the FORCE reached the Schmeague Championship. Membership has more than doubled since the club’s inaugural season in 2023, with the largest single month of growth arriving in December.

About FORCE Nation

Membership is free and open to supporters of all ages. Members receive presale access to single-game tickets, invitations to member-only events, exclusive giveaways and the FORCE Nation newsletter.

“Twenty-five thousand people chose to raise their hand and say they are with this club. That is not something we take lightly.” VP, Community Relations, San Jose FORCE

Members inside the greater San Jose area now account for roughly two-thirds of the total, with the remainder spread across California and beyond.

Joining

Registration takes a few minutes and confirmation arrives by email with a member number. Sign-up is available on the Community page, and 2026 member benefits will be announced in the spring.
