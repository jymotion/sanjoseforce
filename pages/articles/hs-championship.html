General Electric Field will host the regional high school football championship for the first time, the San Jose FORCE announced today.

The game brings together the two top-seeded programs in the region and will be played on the same field the FORCE call home, with full stadium operations including video board, public address and concessions.

Supporting School Programs

The FORCE are waiving venue rental fees for the event. Net proceeds from concessions will be divided between the athletic programs of the two participating schools.

“A lot of these young people have watched games in this building. Getting to play in it is something they will carry with them.” President of Business Operations, San Jose FORCE

Attending

Tickets are sold through the participating schools. Standard General Electric Field policies apply, including the clear bag policy and cashless payment. Parking in Lot A will be free for this event.
