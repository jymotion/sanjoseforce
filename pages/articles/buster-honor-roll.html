Buster, the official mascot of the San Jose FORCE, has been named to the Schmeague Mascot Honor Roll in recognition of outstanding community engagement during the 2025 season.

The Honor Roll recognizes mascots across the league who make a measurable contribution to their communities. Buster is the first San Jose honoree.

A Busy Year

Buster appeared at all seven home games at General Electric Field this season and made more than sixty community appearances, visiting schools, hospitals, youth clinics and neighborhood events throughout the greater San Jose area.

Highlights included leading the team onto the field before every home game, a visit to every elementary school in the club’s Read with Buster literacy program, and an appearance at the club’s holiday food drive earlier this month.

“Buster is at the centre of everything we do in this community. This recognition is well earned.” VP, Community Relations, San Jose FORCE

Requesting an Appearance

Buster is available for community appearances throughout the year. Requests should be submitted at least fourteen days in advance through the Community page.
