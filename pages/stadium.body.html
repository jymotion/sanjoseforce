Plan Your Visit

General Electric Field sits just off the freeway at 1 Partnership Way, a short walk from the light rail station and easily reached by car, bike or transit. However you get here, we recommend arriving early — the tailgate lots and the plaza outside Gate A are where FORCE Nation really comes alive.

Gates & Entry

Gates open ninety minutes before kickoff. All guests pass through security screening, so give yourself a little extra time on busy game days. Digital tickets can be scanned straight from your phone at any gate.

Parking

Parking is available in Lots A through D, with tailgating permitted in Lots C and D beginning when the lots open four hours before kickoff. Passes sell out for most home games, so we recommend buying in advance through Account Manager. Accessible parking is available in Lot A with a valid placard.

Bag Policy

To keep entry lines moving, General Electric Field uses a clear bag policy:

  • One clear plastic bag no larger than 12″ × 6″ × 12″, or
  • One one-gallon clear resealable storage bag, plus
  • One small clutch or wallet no larger than 4.5″ × 6.5″

Bags that don’t meet these guidelines can’t be brought inside, and no bag check is available on site, so please plan ahead. Medical and diaper bags are always welcome after screening.

Concessions

You’ll find South Bay favorites throughout both concourses — garlic fries on the 200 level, tri-tip sandwiches behind sections 118 and 134, and a rotating local taco stand near Gate C. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are marked on every menu board, and every stand offers a $5 value item.

Accessibility

Accessible seating, restrooms and parking are available throughout the venue, along with assistive listening devices and sensory kits available at any Guest Services location. Please reach out ahead of your visit and we’ll make sure everything is ready for you.

Guest Services

Guest Services is located on both concourse levels near sections 108 and 232. Stop by anytime — our team is happy to help with seating, accessibility, lost items or directions.

A–Z Guide

Presented byDeloitte
Alcohol
Beer, wine and seltzer are available to guests 21 and over with valid ID. Sales end at the conclusion of the third quarter. Local breweries are featured at the taps near sections 114 and 226.
Cameras
Personal cameras are welcome. Lenses longer than six inches, tripods and monopods aren’t permitted. Media credentials are issued through the Communications Department.
First Aid
First aid stations are located on both concourse levels and staffed by licensed medical personnel throughout every event. Any staff member can radio for help.
Lost & Found
Items found during an event are turned in to Guest Services. After game day, contact us at lostandfound@sanjoseforce.com. Unclaimed items are held for thirty days.
Mascot Appearances
Buster appears at every home game and visits community events across the region. Submit appearance requests at least fourteen days in advance.
Prohibited Items
Weapons, outside food and beverage, glass containers, umbrellas, noisemakers, drones, poles and banners larger than 3′ × 5′ are not permitted. When in doubt, leave it in the car.
Sensory Support
Sensory kits with noise-reducing headphones and fidget tools are available at Guest Services, and a quiet sensory room is located on the main concourse behind section 122.
Wi-Fi
Free guest Wi-Fi is available throughout the venue. Connect to GEFIELD-GUEST — no password required.
