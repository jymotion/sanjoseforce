Ticketing FAQ

How do I access my tickets?
All tickets are digital. Sign in to Account Manager on your phone and add them to your Apple or Google Wallet before you head to the stadium — cell service gets busy near the gates. Screenshots and printouts can’t be scanned.
Can I transfer my tickets?
Yes, free of charge. Send them through Account Manager and your guest will get an email to claim them. Transfers can be recalled any time before they’re accepted.
What is the refund policy?
Single game tickets are non-refundable. If an event is cancelled outright, you’ll be refunded automatically to your original payment method.
Is the venue cashless?
Yes. General Electric Field accepts credit, debit and mobile payment everywhere, including parking. Reverse ATMs near Gates A and C convert cash to a prepaid card at no charge.
What if a game is postponed?
Your tickets will be honored for the rescheduled date. We’ll email everyone affected as soon as a new date is confirmed.
