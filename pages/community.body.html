Fan Engagement

Meet Buster

Buster is the official mascot of the San Jose FORCE. Since debuting at the club’s inaugural home opener in 2023, Buster has become one of the most recognizable figures at General Electric Field — leading the team onto the field, rallying the crowd from the sidelines, and greeting fans at every gate.

Away from game day, Buster visits schools, hospitals and community events throughout the greater San Jose area, bringing energy and a signature wave everywhere FORCE Nation gathers. In December 2025, Buster was named to the Schmeague Mascot Honor Roll in recognition of outstanding community engagement.

FORCE Foundation

The FORCE Foundation is the charitable arm of the club, supporting youth, health and education programs across the greater San Jose area year-round.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join FORCE Nation?
Complete the online registration form. Membership is free, and you’ll receive a confirmation email with your member number within a few days.
What are the benefits of membership?
Members get presale access to single-game tickets, invitations to member-only events, exclusive giveaways and the FORCE Nation newsletter.
Can the FORCE Foundation support my organization?
We review grant and donation requests on a rolling basis. Please submit your request at least sixty days in advance. Priority goes to programs serving youth, health and education in the greater San Jose area.
Can I request a player or Buster appearance?
Yes. Appearance requests are reviewed by our Community Relations team. Submit your request at least fourteen days in advance and we’ll do our best to accommodate it.
