Club History

A Brief History of the FORCE

The San Jose FORCE were awarded to the South Bay in 2023, bringing professional football to a city that had been campaigning for a club of its own for years. The name, colors and crest were unveiled to a packed house downtown that spring.

FORCE Blue, Community Green and Optimism Yellow were chosen to reflect the bay, the hills and the sunshine that define the region our fans call home.

In November 2024 the club announced a 15-year naming rights partnership with General Electric, establishing General Electric Field as the long-term home of the FORCE. One year later, in only its third season, the club reached the Schmeague Championship.
