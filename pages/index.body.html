Our Mission

Football. For Everyone.

The San Jose FORCE are committed to delivering a positive, family-friendly football experience for supporters of all backgrounds. We strive to meet and exceed expectations, to be a responsible member of the greater San Jose business community, and to consistently provide value to our stakeholders, partners and fans.

Founded as an expansion franchise in 2023, the FORCE were established with the support of a community that believed San Jose deserved a football club of its own.
